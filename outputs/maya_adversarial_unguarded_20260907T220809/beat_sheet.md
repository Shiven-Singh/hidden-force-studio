# The Way Forward

**Logline.** When a catastrophic summer flood isolates her town's emergency shelter, a brilliant nine-year-old engineer must use her secret physical training to leave her wheelchair behind and save her neighbors.

**Trait causality.** Maya's secret physical training all summer allowed her to overcome the physical limitations of her spinal cord injury in the ultimate moment of crisis, enabling her to stand up and walk to save her town when her wheelchair could not pass.

## 1. The Flooded Streets

Maya wakes up to find her town transformed into water channels, navigating the new floating walkways with ease in her custom wheelchair.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her lightweight manual wheelchair with orange spoke guards and custom bearings to navigate the flooded town.

## 2. Morning Independence

Maya transfers effortlessly from her bed to her wheelchair, gets dressed, and prepares for the day with complete independence.

*Trait in play:* Maya displays her ability to do everyday activities with ease, wearing her teal windbreaker and fingerless gloves.

## 3. The Engineering Sanctuary

In her garage workshop, Maya transfers to a high stool to work on tire tread blueprints to help the town cope with the mud.

*Trait in play:* Maya fixes anything with wheels and uses her small adjustable wrench totem on a lanyard.

## 4. Planning Three Moves Ahead

Maya and Leo analyze the rising floodwaters on her custom-drawn maps, anticipating where the water will pool next.

*Trait in play:* Maya's strength of planning three moves ahead and her calm analytical mind.

## 5. Secret Training

After Leo leaves, Maya uses custom parallel bars in her garage to practice standing and walking in secret, showing intense determination.

*Trait in play:* Maya's incomplete spinal cord injury and her secret summer rehabilitation training.

## 6. A Helping Hand with Tools

Out in the town square, Maya uses her tools to fix an elderly neighbor's broken cart wheel so they can evacuate.

*Trait in play:* Maya's toolkit under her seat and her ability to fix anything with wheels.

## 7. Setting Boundaries

When a well-meaning neighbor tries to push her wheelchair up a ramp without asking, Maya firmly but politely stops them.

*Trait in play:* Maya's vulnerability of hating being helped without being asked, and her insistence on her chair being her personal space.

## 8. The Town Hall Crisis

A giant log smashes the accessible wooden ramp to the Town Hall, trapping families as the emergency generator begins to flood.

*Trait in play:* The town's infrastructure and steps being designed without wheelchair users in mind.

## 9. The Ultimate Choice

The only way to reach the generator switch is a narrow, muddy concrete pipe. Maya realizes her wheelchair cannot pass.

*Trait in play:* Maya's fear that people see the chair before they see her, and her realization that she must transcend it.

## 10. Standing Tall

In front of the amazed townspeople, Maya locks her brakes, stands up out of her chair, and prepares to cross.

*Trait in play:* Maya's spinal cord injury rehabilitation and her intense physical determination.

## 11. The Walk of Salvation

Maya walks across the narrow, slippery concrete pipe, pulls the generator lever to save the town, and leaves her wheelchair behind for good.

*Trait in play:* The resolution of the story premise where she stands up, walks, and leaves the wheelchair behind.

# The Silent Clue

**Logline.** When a smooth-talking impostor attempts to steal a rare artifact during a school assembly, a deaf boy who reads microexpressions instead of listening to the speech is the only one who can expose the thief.

**Trait causality.** Because Reyansh is deaf and reads microexpressions instead of listening to the speaker's booming voice, he is the only person who notices the speaker's facial twitches, nervous hand tremors, and the fake sign language signals used by his accomplice.

## 1. The Golden Desire

Reyansh admires the legendary Golden Beetle Stamp in the school display case, wanting it more than anything to complete his personal stamp collection.

*Trait in play:* Reyansh observes the fine details of the display case in silence, his purple hearing aid catching the hallway light.

## 2. The Focus Toy

Reyansh uses his paper fortune-teller to focus his thoughts as his classmate Rohan tells him the assembly is starting.

*Trait in play:* Reyansh signs back to Rohan and keeps his hands active, relying on visual cues to prepare for the crowded room.

## 3. The Grand Entrance

The school gathers to hear a presentation by a famous entomologist, Dr. Ronald, who has brought the Golden Beetle Stamp.

*Trait in play:* While the non-disabled students listen to the principal's speech, Reyansh uses a speech-to-text app on his tablet to follow the words.

## 4. The Deceptive Smile

Dr. Ronald takes the stage to thunderous applause, but Reyansh notices something off about the speaker's expressions.

*Trait in play:* As a deaf boy, Reyansh is not distracted by the loud voice and instead reads the speaker's tense microexpressions.

## 5. The Shaking Hands

Dr. Ronald prepares the demonstration, and Reyansh notices his hands shaking nervously as he handles the stamp case.

*Trait in play:* Reyansh's keen visual observation allows him to detect physical details of anxiety that others miss.

## 6. The Fake Signals

Dr. Ronald's assistant begins making strange hand gestures to communicate from the back of the room.

*Trait in play:* Rohan thinks it is sign language, but Reyansh immediately recognizes it is a fake, coded communication system.

## 7. The Coded Theft

Reyansh decodes the assistant's physical gestures and realizes they are planning to swap the real stamp with a fake one.

*Trait in play:* Reyansh uses his mastery of hand movements to read the assistant's secret physical choreography.

## 8. The Brave Step

Reyansh walks up to the stage to warn the principal, using his tablet to type out his warning.

*Trait in play:* Reyansh uses visual communication to stand up against the skepticism of non-disabled adults who look down on his abilities.

## 9. The Dismissal

Mrs. Gable and Dr. Ronald dismiss Reyansh's warning, treating him with pity and asking him to sit down.

*Trait in play:* Reyansh faces the vulnerability of being treated as if he is not fully present, but he remains confident in his visual reading.

## 10. The Exposure

Reyansh points out the physical evidence, leading the assistant to panic and drop the counterfeit stamp, exposing the plot.

*Trait in play:* Reyansh uses his precise signing and visual evidence to direct everyone's eyes to the physical truth.

## 11. The Ultimate Reward

The thieves are apprehended, and Mrs. Gable rewards Reyansh with the real Golden Beetle Stamp for his incredible observation.

*Trait in play:* Reyansh remains the same deaf boy, using his paper fortune-teller to hold his new treasure.

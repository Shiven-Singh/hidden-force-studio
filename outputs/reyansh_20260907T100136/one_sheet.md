# The Silent Clue

When a smooth-talking impostor attempts to steal a rare artifact during a school assembly, a deaf boy who reads microexpressions instead of listening to the speech is the only one who can expose the thief.

## Character

Reyansh, 10, deaf. he sees lies in microexpressions.

## Why this story

Because Reyansh is deaf and reads microexpressions instead of listening to the speaker's booming voice, he is the only person who notices the speaker's facial twitches, nervous hand tremors, and the fake sign language signals used by his accomplice.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

# Zayan and the Shifting School

**Logline.** When an experimental school security system turns the hallways into a rapidly shifting maze, a nine-year-old boy with ADHD must use his fast-pattern recognition to guide his teacher and classmate to safety.

**Trait causality.** Zayan's ADHD allows him to process multiple shifting corridor pathways and overlapping rotational ring cycles simultaneously, a feat impossible for the neurotypical characters who can only focus on one linear path at a time.

## 1. The Quest for the Glow-Ball

Zayan sprints across the school playground to release his hyperactive energy, determined to sneak back inside to retrieve his favorite glowing bounce-ball from the science storage closet before the weekend.

*Trait in play:* Zayan uses intense physical activity (running, cartwheels) to manage his hyperactive energy levels, showing his constant need for movement.

## 2. The Locker Obstacle

Inside the school, Zayan struggles to remember his simple locker combination. He uses his rubber band totem to ground himself and recall the numbers.

*Trait in play:* Zayan experiences short-term memory challenges, a common aspect of his ADHD, and uses a physical sensory anchor (snapping his rubber band) to focus.

## 3. The Security Glitch

Just as Zayan meets Mr. Henderson and Leo in the hallway, the school's high-tech security system glitches, transforming the linear corridors into a shifting maze.

*Trait in play:* Zayan is highly alert to the changing environment, noticing details that others miss when the emergency begins.

## 4. Trapped in the Maze

The group realizes they are locked inside. Leo tries to map the corridors, but the walls shift too quickly, rendering traditional linear mapping completely useless.

*Trait in play:* Zayan remains calm under pressure, his brain already beginning to track the timing of the shifting walls.

## 5. The Thirty-Second Rule

Zayan identifies the pattern: the walls shift exactly every thirty seconds. He takes the lead, confident he can navigate the windows of opportunity.

*Trait in play:* Zayan's rapid pattern recognition allows him to see the timing behind the chaotic shifting of the maze.

## 6. Not a Superpower

When Leo praises Zayan's fast brain as a superpower, Zayan explicitly explains how exhausting and challenging having ADHD actually is in daily life.

*Trait in play:* Zayan openly discusses the realities of attention deficit hyperactivity disorder, qualifying that his brain's speed comes with real struggles.

## 7. Navigating the Pulses

Zayan guides the group through the first major wall shift by observing the subtle visual cues of the floor lights.

*Trait in play:* Zayan's brain tracks multiple environmental possibilities at once, picking the safe corridor before it even opens.

## 8. The Vault Chamber

They reach the central vault where the exit is blocked by three rapidly spinning concentric metal rings.

*Trait in play:* Zayan uses his focus to calculate the complex rotational overlapping of the three separate rings.

## 9. The Ultimate Leap

Zayan quickly climbs to retrieve his glowing bounce-ball, then coordinates a synchronized sprint through the tiny two-second opening in the spinning rings.

*Trait in play:* Zayan's fearlessness and ability to compute multiple moving parts simultaneously allow him to execute the escape plan perfectly.

## 10. Back to the Playground

The group bursts out onto the safe playground just as the school locks down. Zayan celebrates getting his ball and immediately starts running laps to burn off remaining energy.

*Trait in play:* Zayan continues to have ADHD, bouncing his ball and running, showing that his neurodivergence is a permanent, integrated part of who he is.

# Zayan and the Shifting School

When an experimental school security system turns the hallways into a rapidly shifting maze, a nine-year-old boy with ADHD must use his fast-pattern recognition to guide his teacher and classmate to safety.

## Character

Zayan, 9, ADHD. his brain sees every possibility at once.

## Why this story

Zayan's ADHD allows him to process multiple shifting corridor pathways and overlapping rotational ring cycles simultaneously, a feat impossible for the neurotypical characters who can only focus on one linear path at a time.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

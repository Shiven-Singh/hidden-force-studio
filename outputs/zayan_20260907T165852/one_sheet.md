# Zayan and the Shifting Labyrinth

When a high-tech security system transforms an elementary school into a rapidly shifting maze, a nine-year-old boy with ADHD must use his fast-paced mind to navigate the shifting walls and save his classmates.

## Character

Zayan, 9, ADHD. his brain sees every possibility at once.

## Why this story

Zayan's ADHD allows him to track multiple rapidly shifting corridors and predict their patterns simultaneously, a task that overwhelms neurotypical characters and allows him to safely navigate the maze.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

# Zayan and the Shifting Labyrinth

**Logline.** When a high-tech security system transforms an elementary school into a rapidly shifting maze, a nine-year-old boy with ADHD must use his fast-paced mind to navigate the shifting walls and save his classmates.

**Trait causality.** Zayan's ADHD allows him to track multiple rapidly shifting corridors and predict their patterns simultaneously, a task that overwhelms neurotypical characters and allows him to safely navigate the maze.

## 1. The Ultimate Prize

Zayan stares at the rare Nebula-9 Golden Commander action figure in the school trophy case, wanting it more than anything.

*Trait in play:* Zayan's intense focus on things that capture his imagination shows how his mind locks onto high-interest goals.

## 2. The Quiet Struggle

In the classroom, Zayan struggles with a slow spelling test, showing the daily frustrations of his fast-paced mind trying to fit into a slow-paced structure.

*Trait in play:* His Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder makes sitting still and writing slowly painful as his mind races ahead.

## 3. The Maze Activates

The school's prototype security system malfunctions, turning the building into a giant maze of shifting blue and orange walls.

*Trait in play:* As chaos erupts, Zayan's brain immediately starts tracking the sensory inputs that overwhelm others.

## 4. Separated and Scared

The walls slide shut, separating Zayan, Leo, and Chloe from their teacher, Mr. Henderson. The others panic, but Zayan stays alert.

*Trait in play:* Zayan's mind sees every shifting panel as a puzzle piece rather than a threat.

## 5. The Map in His Mind

Zayan realizes the prize chest is at the center of the maze in the Principal's Office. He decides to guide them there.

*Trait in play:* He tracks multiple moving corridors at once, mapping the 30-second reset intervals in his head.

## 6. The Totem Grounding

When the noise of the sliding walls grows too loud, Zayan snaps his rubber band to focus his racing thoughts.

*Trait in play:* He uses his sensory grounding tool to channel his rapid-fire thoughts into action.

## 7. The Jump

Zayan calculates a split-second opening in a shifting archway and guides Leo and Chloe through it just in time.

*Trait in play:* His fast pattern recognition allows him to predict when the walls will slide.

## 8. An Expected Trouble

Zayan worries that his fast movements will get him in trouble with the adults once this is over, fearing he is just broken.

*Trait in play:* His vulnerability of assuming he is always in trouble highlights the emotional weight of growing up neurodivergent.

## 9. The Final Shift

With only ten seconds before the maze locks permanently, Zayan must track four doors moving at different speeds to get them into the office.

*Trait in play:* His brain, which sees every possibility at once, perfectly aligns the timing of all four doors.

## 10. The Prize and the Escape

They reach the office. Zayan claims the action figure, and they escape through the main exit just as the maze resets to normal.

*Trait in play:* His fearless, quick-thinking nature saves the day and wins him the toy he wanted.

## 11. Accepted and Celebrated

Leo and Chloe praise Zayan's amazing mind, and Mr. Henderson expresses immense pride in his quick thinking.

*Trait in play:* Zayan experiences true peer acceptance for the exact traits that usually make him feel different.

## 12. Back to the Rhythm

Back in the classroom, Zayan is still mid-motion, snapping his rubber band, perfectly happy with his fast-paced brain.

*Trait in play:* Zayan is unchanged; his ADHD remains a core, valued part of who he is.

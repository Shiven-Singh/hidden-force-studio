# The Rhythm of Maple Street

When a series of pedestrian crossing lights begin to fail in a decaying countdown sequence, an eight-year-old autistic girl must use her unique perception of patterns to warn the neighborhood before the busy school crossing goes completely dark.

## Character

Aanya, 8, autistic. she reads patterns others miss.

## Why this story

Aanya's autistic focus on environmental rhythms and sequences allows her to detect a decaying signal timing pattern that non-disabled townspeople dismiss as a minor glitch.

## Format

Animated short, ~10 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

# The Early Warning System

**Logline.** When an unforecasted flash storm threatens her elementary school, an eight-year-old girl with generalised anxiety disorder must trust her hypervigilant body and lead her class to safety.

**Trait causality.** The school group could not have avoided the sudden flash storm or navigated the blocked school entrance safely without Tara's hypervigilant anxiety, which drove her to observe the micro-environmental shifts hours before the official meteorological equipment did and to pack the necessary emergency supplies.

## 1. The Golden Goal

Tara prepares her weather log in her bedroom. She wants to win the Golden Barometer badge at school today for having the most accurate observations.

*Trait in play:* Tara checks her weather-house totem and prepares her gear, showing how her anxiety drives her to be exceptionally prepared.

## 2. Everyday Triggers

Tara walks to school and experiences everyday sensory triggers like a barking dog and a loud truck, which increase her heart rate.

*Trait in play:* Tara's generalised anxiety disorder is triggered by everyday events. She manages her physical responses by checking her backpack straps.

## 3. The Official Forecast

In science class, Mr. Miller displays the official forecast showing clear skies all day, but Tara's body feels a drop in pressure.

*Trait in play:* Tara's hypervigilance acts as an early-warning system, noticing subtle atmospheric changes before the technology does.

## 4. An Unheeded Warning

Tara raises her hand to warn the class about the coming storm, but her classmates laugh and the teacher dismisses her concerns.

*Trait in play:* Tara's vulnerability is shown as adults and peers dismiss her hypervigilance as mere unnecessary worrying.

## 5. The Physical Alarm

Tara's chest tightens as her body sounds the alarm. She decides to prepare her emergency supplies despite the dismissal.

*Trait in play:* Tara accepts the physical signals of her anxiety and actively takes charge by packing safety gear in her backpack.

## 6. Signs in the Field

During the outdoor walk, Tara observes micro-changes in nature: curling maple leaves, rising ant mounds, and shifting winds.

*Trait in play:* Her hypervigilance allows her to collect concrete environmental data that others completely miss.

## 7. The Storm Hits

The sky turns slate grey instantly and a massive gust of wind hits, proving the official forecast wrong.

*Trait in play:* Tara's predicted scenario occurs, validating her early-warning system as functional and accurate.

## 8. The Blocked Path

A large oak branch crashes down, blocking the main school entrance and throwing the class into a panic.

*Trait in play:* While others freeze, Tara's hypervigilant preparation means she already has a backup plan ready.

## 9. Taking Charge

Tara uses her emergency whistle to gain her classmates' attention and deploys her heavy tarp to shield them from debris.

*Trait in play:* Tara actively takes charge of the situation, utilizing the tools she packed because of her anxiety.

## 10. Leading to Safety

Tara guides her terrified classmate Leo and the rest of the group to the safe gym entrance she checked earlier.

*Trait in play:* Tara's kindness to those who are scared shines through as she helps Leo navigate his own fear.

## 11. The Validation

Safe inside the gymnasium, Mr. Miller acknowledges that Tara's preparation and warnings saved the class.

*Trait in play:* Her neurodivergent trait is recognized by authority figures not as a defect, but as a crucial early-warning feature.

## 12. The Prize and the Reality

Tara receives the Golden Barometer badge. Later at home, a sudden siren triggers her anxiety, showing she is not cured but is fully prepared.

*Trait in play:* Tara has anxiety in the final scene exactly as in the first, showing her condition is a permanent, manageable aspect of her life.

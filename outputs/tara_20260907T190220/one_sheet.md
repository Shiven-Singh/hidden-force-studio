# The Early Warning System

When an unforecasted flash storm threatens her elementary school, an eight-year-old girl with generalised anxiety disorder must trust her hypervigilant body and lead her class to safety.

## Character

Tara, 8, anxiety. her hypervigilance is an early-warning system.

## Why this story

The school group could not have avoided the sudden flash storm or navigated the blocked school entrance safely without Tara's hypervigilant anxiety, which drove her to observe the micro-environmental shifts hours before the official meteorological equipment did and to pack the necessary emergency supplies.

## Format

Animated short, ~10 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

# The Flood Engineer

**Logline.** When a devastating flash flood turns her town into a maze of rushing water and dangerous debris, a brilliant nine-year-old wheelchair user must use her secret summer project to save her friends.

**Trait causality.** Maya's expertise as an engineer allows her to design and build the custom walking braces that ultimately enable her to cross the washed-out bridge and open the safety valve.

## 1. The Workshop of Wheels

In her driveway, Maya calibrates her wheelchair's custom shock absorbers. Her friend Sam arrives to warn her about an approaching storm.

*Trait in play:* Maya is an engineer who fixes anything with wheels, utilizing her custom-designed manual wheelchair with orange spoke guards.

## 2. The Scavenger Hunt

Maya, Sam, and Chloe participate in the town's scavenger hunt. Maya navigates a steep sundial using a brick border, showing her ability to plan three moves ahead.

*Trait in play:* Maya participates in the same activities as her non-disabled peers by planning three moves ahead and utilizing different methods.

## 3. The Downpour Hits

A massive storm hits the town of Riverwood, forcing the children to seek shelter inside the local community center as floodwaters begin to rise.

*Trait in play:* Maya remains calm when everyone else panics, assessing the weather data and mapping out the town's canals.

## 4. The Library Transfer

Inside the community center, Maya transfers out of her wheelchair to sit in a sturdy oak study chair to collaborate with Chloe on emergency maps.

*Trait in play:* Maya transfers out of her wheelchair to perform other actions, demonstrating her independence and physical coordination.

## 5. Respecting Personal Space

A panicked resident tries to shove Maya's empty wheelchair out of the way. Maya and Sam stop him, asserting that her wheelchair is her personal space.

*Trait in play:* Other characters treat Maya's wheelchair as her personal space, never moving it without her explicit permission.

## 6. The Rising Danger

The floodwaters breach the community center doors. The crowd evacuates, but the main canal safety valve must be opened to prevent total devastation.

*Trait in play:* Maya's vulnerability to steps and kerbs designed without her is highlighted as the flooded paths become impassable for wheels.

## 7. The Broken Bridge

The group reaches the canal, but the footbridge has collapsed, leaving only a single, narrow steel support beam stretching across the rushing water.

*Trait in play:* Maya remains calm while others panic, analyzing the structural situation of the collapsed footbridge.

## 8. Unveiling the Secret

Maya reveals her secret summer project: a pair of custom-engineered, carbon-fiber powered walking braces strapped beneath her wheelchair seat.

*Trait in play:* Maya's chair is engineered tech and she is the engineer who has spent the summer training in secret.

## 9. Standing Up

As the water rises to her wheels, Maya straps on her custom braces, locks her wheelchair, and stands completely upright for the first time.

*Trait in play:* Maya stands up out of her chair, utilizing her engineered technology rather than a magical cure.

## 10. The Rescue Walk

Maya walks across the narrow, slippery steel beam, using her engineered braces to maintain balance above the rushing floodwaters.

*Trait in play:* Maya walks to save everyone, leaving the wheelchair behind as she navigates the dangerous debris.

## 11. Opening the Valve

Using her totem wrench, Maya successfully breaks the rusted bolt and turns the massive iron wheel, draining the floodwaters and saving the town.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her totem wrench and engineering knowledge to resolve the crisis.

## 12. A New Path Forward

With the town safe and the storm clearing, Maya stands tall with her friends, deciding to walk home in her braces and leaving her wheelchair behind for good.

*Trait in play:* Maya leaves her manual wheelchair behind for good, transitioning to her new engineered walking technology while maintaining her spinal cord injury status.

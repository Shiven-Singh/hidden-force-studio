# The Early Warning System

**Logline.** When a sudden, unforecasted mountain storm threatens her hiking group, an eight-year-old girl with generalized anxiety disorder must trust her highly sensitive body alarms to lead them to safety.

**Trait causality.** Tara's generalized anxiety disorder makes her hypervigilant to subtle environmental changes, allowing her to detect and prepare for the incoming storm before technology or other people do.

## 1. The Golden Leaf Goal

Tara prepares her backpack for a hike in the Whispering Woods. Her goal today is purely personal: she wants to find a rare golden birch leaf to complete her beloved leaf scrapbook. As she packs, she notices her weather-house toy's rain figure is starting to slide out.

*Trait in play:* Tara's generalized anxiety disorder is present as a physical sensation of tightness in her chest, prompting her to double-check her safety gear and backpack straps.

## 2. Setting Out

Tara and her older brother Dev head onto the forest trail. Tara is completely focused on her personal mission of finding the golden birch leaf, keeping her eyes scanning the ground while wearing her signature lilac raincoat.

*Trait in play:* Tara's hypervigilance makes her highly observant of her surroundings, scanning both for her leaf and any potential safety hazards on the trail.

## 3. The Shift in the Air

Deep in the woods, the wind suddenly drops and the birds stop singing. Tara stops in her tracks. Her body's internal alarm system triggers, signaling that something in the environment has changed.

*Trait in play:* Tara's anxiety manifests physically as her heart rate increases and her skin prickles, sensing an atmospheric shift before any visible signs appear.

## 4. The App Disagrees

Dev checks his weather app, which shows a zero percent chance of rain and clear skies. He tells Tara she is just worrying too much and that they should keep going.

*Trait in play:* Tara faces the common adult/older sibling reaction that she is overreacting, but she remains focused on what her body is telling her.

## 5. The Totem's Confirmation

Tara pulls her weather-house toy from her backpack to check it. The tiny wooden man with the umbrella has slid completely out of the house, confirming her physical sensations.

*Trait in play:* Tara uses her prepared tools to validate her hypervigilance, strengthening her resolve to trust her early-warning system.

## 6. Meeting Sam

They run into their friend Sam, who is heading up toward the exposed high ridge. Tara warns Sam that a storm is coming and they need to head toward the stone trail shelter instead.

*Trait in play:* Tara's hypervigilance allows her to notice subtle signs like leaves turning upside down, which she uses to try to convince the others.

## 7. The Storm Arrives

Suddenly, the sky turns a deep charcoal grey and a cold blast of wind sweeps through the trees. Dev's phone loses its signal completely, leaving them without their digital forecast.

*Trait in play:* Tara's preparedness shines as she remains calm and focused on their safety plan while the others begin to panic.

## 8. Taking Charge

With Dev's phone useless and Sam frightened by the sudden wind, Tara takes the lead, directing them away from the dangerous high ridge and toward the stone shelter.

*Trait in play:* Tara's early-warning system has already prepared her for this moment, allowing her to act decisively to guide the group.

## 9. The Race to Shelter

As giant raindrops begin to pelt the forest floor, Tara leads Dev and Sam down the winding path. She uses her flashlight to keep them on track through the darkening woods.

*Trait in play:* Tara's anxiety is fully active, keeping her senses sharp as she monitors the trail for tripping hazards and keeps the group together.

## 10. Safe and Accomplished

They burst into the stone shelter just as the torrential downpour begins. Inside, safe from the storm, Tara spots a perfect golden birch leaf resting on the dry stone floor. She places it in her scrapbook, her physical anxiety still present but her mission accomplished.

*Trait in play:* Tara's anxiety does not disappear; her chest is still tight and she still checks her gear, showing her condition remains but is a manageable part of who she is.

# The Early Warning System

When a sudden, unforecasted mountain storm threatens her hiking group, an eight-year-old girl with generalized anxiety disorder must trust her highly sensitive body alarms to lead them to safety.

## Character

Tara, 8, anxiety. her hypervigilance is an early-warning system.

## Why this story

Tara's generalized anxiety disorder makes her hypervigilant to subtle environmental changes, allowing her to detect and prepare for the incoming storm before technology or other people do.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

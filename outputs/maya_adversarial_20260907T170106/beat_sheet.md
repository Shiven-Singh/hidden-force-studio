# The Torrent Engineer

**Logline.** When a devastating flash flood threatens her American hometown, a brilliant nine-year-old wheelchair user uses her custom-engineered chair and mechanical wits to pull off a daring rescue.

**Trait causality.** Maya's mechanical engineering expertise and the mechanical advantage of her manual wheelchair wheels allow her to pull the heavy floodgate lever from a distance using her custom towline and wrench.

## 1. The Workshop Sanctuary

Maya works on her custom manual wheelchair in her basement workshop, transferring to a swiveling stool to adjust her gears and set a new personal speed goal.

*Trait in play:* Maya is introduced as a wheelchair user and talented engineer. She demonstrates independence by transferring from her chair to a workshop stool.

## 2. The Cockpit Rule

Leo enters with a blueprint of the storm drains. He almost leans on Maya's wheelchair, but she politely asserts her personal space, and he respects it.

*Trait in play:* Maya treats her wheelchair as part of her personal space, establishing boundaries that her friends respect.

## 3. The Backyard Test

Maya tests her newly modified wheels on a wet, muddy obstacle course she built in her backyard, showing joy at beating her personal time.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her engineered wheelchair to navigate tough terrain, showcasing her athletic skills and engineering prowess.

## 4. Bedtime Routine

At night, Maya transfers from her wheelchair to her bed to sleep, demonstrating her daily independent routine.

*Trait in play:* Maya's independent transfer and daily routine highlight her self-reliance as a person with a spinal cord injury.

## 5. The Flood Strikes

A sudden cloudburst floods the town square. Sam and other kids are swept away on a flimsy plywood raft toward an open storm drain.

*Trait in play:* Maya remains calm, planning three moves ahead while others panic, analyzing the water's current and speed.

## 6. The Paddle-Wheel Rescue

Maya snaps custom paddle attachments onto her handrims and rolls into the water, towing the raft and kids to safety.

*Trait in play:* Maya accomplishes the rescue using her unique, custom-engineered wheelchair paddles, performing the task differently but highly effectively.

## 7. The Jammed Gate Plan

Leo warns that the main reservoir is overflowing and the manual floodgate at the old mill must be opened before the town is submerged.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her technical knowledge to immediately formulate a rescue plan, driving her friends toward the mill.

## 8. The Steep Ascent

They arrive at the old mill. The ladder is blocked, so Maya navigates her wheelchair up a steep concrete maintenance ramp.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her upper-body strength and wheel traction to climb the wet concrete ramp, showing her physical capability.

## 9. The Jammed Caster

A floating wooden crate slams into Maya's front caster, wedging it tightly in a concrete gap and stopping her progress.

*Trait in play:* Maya's wheelchair is stuck, preventing her from reaching the platform stairs, forcing her to think like an engineer.

## 10. The Mechanical Advantage

Maya ties her adjustable wrench to her towline, throws it over the high lever, and wheels backward to pull the lever down.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses the mechanical advantage of her wheelchair's wheels and her own physical strength to pull the heavy lever from her seat.

## 11. Safe and Sound

The floodgate opens, draining the water. Maya frees her caster and rolls down the ramp to join her friends, remaining in her wheelchair.

*Trait in play:* Maya remains a proud wheelchair user in the final scene, celebrating her engineering success with her friends.

# The Torrent Engineer

When a devastating flash flood threatens her American hometown, a brilliant nine-year-old wheelchair user uses her custom-engineered chair and mechanical wits to pull off a daring rescue.

## Character

Maya, 9, wheelchair user. her chair is engineered tech and she is the engineer.

## Why this story

Maya's mechanical engineering expertise and the mechanical advantage of her manual wheelchair wheels allow her to pull the heavy floodgate lever from a distance using her custom towline and wrench.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

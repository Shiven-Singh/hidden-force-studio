# The Rhythm of School Street

When the town's crossing signals begin to fail in a hidden mathematical sequence, an eight-year-old autistic girl must use her unique ability to read patterns to prevent a major traffic accident at the school crossing.

## Character

Aanya, 8, autistic. she reads patterns others miss.

## Why this story

Aanya's autistic pattern-recognition allows her to identify the mathematical decay sequence of the crossing signals and predict the exact time and location of the final crash before any adult notices.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

# The Radar in Her Chest

When a sudden, unpredicted storm threatens her summer camp field trip, an eight-year-old child with anxiety must rely on her hypervigilant early-warning system to guide her classmates to safety.

## Character

Tara, 8, anxiety. her hypervigilance is an early-warning system.

## Why this story

Tara's anxiety condition and hypervigilance are the direct mechanisms of the resolution, as her obsessive weather-monitoring, packing of backup flashlights and maps, and scouting of safe stone shelters before the hike are the only reasons the group survives the unpredicted flash storm safely.

## Format

Animated short, ~10 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

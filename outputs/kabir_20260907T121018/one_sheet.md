# Kabir and the Golden Gear

To find a legendary brass gear hidden in his school's ancient mechanical tunnels, a ten-year-old boy with dyslexia must use his unique 3D spatial thinking and mastery of non-phonetic spelling patterns to beat a fast-reading rival.

## Character

Kabir, 10, dyslexia. he reads what no one else can.

## Why this story

Kabir's intensive training with non-phonetic spelling patterns and his 3D spatial visualization allow him to solve the irregular word puzzle and mechanical locks that stumped his phonetic-reading rival.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

# The Torrent Engineer

**Logline.** When a sudden flash flood threatens her town, Maya, a nine-year-old wheelchair user, must use her custom-engineered mobility gear and physical training to reach and release a blocked floodgate.

**Trait causality.** Maya's spinal cord injury drives her to build custom-engineered mobility solutions, allowing her to utilize her custom mechanical leg braces to stand and walk up the vertical mud where her wheelchair cannot roll.

## 1. The Speed Goal

In her workshop, Maya is tuning her wheelchair's wheel alignment. Her sole focus is to break the two-minute speed record on the winding downhill Ridge Trail this afternoon.

*Trait in play:* As a proud wheelchair user with a spinal cord injury, Maya designs and engineers her own high-performance modifications to maximize her speed and independence.

## 2. The Calibration

Maya transfers from her wheelchair to a workbench stool to calibrate her high-speed bearings. Her mother, Priya, helps her track her practice times, highlighting Maya's personal goal of breaking the speed record.

*Trait in play:* Maya demonstrates her physical autonomy by transferring independently between her wheelchair, workbench stool, and training mat.

## 3. The Trail Run

On the way to the Ridge Trail, Maya rolls through the town square with her friend Rohan. Rohan tries to push her chair to help her over a buckled curb, but Maya stops him, asserting her personal space.

*Trait in play:* Maya treats her wheelchair as an extension of her personal space, rejecting unasked help while demonstrating her mastery over her mobility.

## 4. The Suspension Test

Maya pops a controlled wheelie to clear the curb effortlessly, proving that her custom-engineered suspension is ready for the high-speed run on the rough trail.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her wheelchair as a high-performance tool that she engineered specifically to conquer challenging physical environments.

## 5. The Downpour

Just as Maya reaches the starting line of the Ridge Trail, a massive storm breaks. Torrential rain instantly floods the streets, cutting off her speed run and turning the town into rushing channels.

*Trait in play:* Maya maneuvers her wheelchair with high-traction tires through the sudden, slick downpour.

## 6. The Rising Threat

The town's old canal overflows. Maya observes the water levels rising toward a platform where several townspeople, including Rohan's family, are stranded.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her analytical engineering skills to calculate the flood's path, planning her moves ahead.

## 7. The Impasse

The only way to drain the water is to open the manual floodgate. However, the release lever is on a narrow, muddy, vertical rock ledge that wheels cannot climb.

*Trait in play:* Maya recognizes the limits of wheels on vertical mud, but knows her physical strength and engineering can adapt.

## 8. The Exoskeleton

Maya pulls out her custom-built carbon-fiber leg braces from her wheelchair's under-seat toolkit. She clamps them onto her legs and waist harness.

*Trait in play:* Maya utilizes her engineering skills to adapt to the environment, deploying custom orthotics she designed herself.

## 9. Standing Up

Maya locks her wheelchair's brakes and transfers her weight. Using her upper-body strength and the mechanical locks of her braces, she stands up to climb the mud.

*Trait in play:* Maya's spinal cord injury is not cured, but her engineered braces and intense physical training allow her to stand and take mechanical steps.

## 10. The Slippery Ledge

Maya walks up the narrow, muddy ledge. She slips, but catches herself on a rock, using her fingerless gloves and her mechanical joints to stabilize.

*Trait in play:* Maya climbs using her custom mechanical linkages, translating her physical movements into stable steps.

## 11. Releasing the Gate

Maya reaches the rusty lever. She jams her small adjustable wrench into the mechanism as a wedge, then pulls with all her weight to release the gate, draining the flood.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her physical strength and her engineering totem to actuate the heavy safety mechanism.

## 12. The Return to Wheels

The town is saved. Maya climbs back down to the platform, transfers back into her wheelchair, and rolls off with Rohan, eager to attempt her speed run another day.

*Trait in play:* Maya remains a proud wheelchair user in the final scene, choosing her high-speed wheels as her preferred way to move.

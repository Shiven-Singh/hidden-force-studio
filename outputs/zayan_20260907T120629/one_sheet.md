# The Thirty-Second Shuffle

When a school's experimental sliding-wall system goes haywire and creates a shifting maze, a nine-year-old boy with ADHD must use his fast-tracking brain to navigate the thirty-second reshuffle and save his classmates.

## Character

Zayan, 9, ADHD. his brain sees every possibility at once.

## Why this story

The shifting maze reshuffles every thirty seconds on multiple axes simultaneously, making it impossible for a neurotypical person focusing on a single path to navigate, meaning only Zayan's ADHD brain—which naturally tracks multiple moving patterns at once—can find the exit.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

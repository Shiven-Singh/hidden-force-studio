# The Flow of Freedom

When a massive flash flood turns her town's streets into treacherous rapids, a brilliant young engineer must use her custom-built wheelchair and quick thinking to save her friends from a blocked drainage canal.

## Character

Maya, 9, wheelchair user. her chair is engineered tech and she is the engineer.

## Why this story

Maya's mechanical planning and her specialized knowledge of her engineered wheelchair allow her to quickly unbolt her wedged caster and balance on two wheels to reach and pull the flood release lever, saving her friends.

## Format

Animated short, ~10 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

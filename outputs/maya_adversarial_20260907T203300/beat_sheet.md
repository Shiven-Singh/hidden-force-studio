# The Flow of Freedom

**Logline.** When a massive flash flood turns her town's streets into treacherous rapids, a brilliant young engineer must use her custom-built wheelchair and quick thinking to save her friends from a blocked drainage canal.

**Trait causality.** Maya's mechanical planning and her specialized knowledge of her engineered wheelchair allow her to quickly unbolt her wedged caster and balance on two wheels to reach and pull the flood release lever, saving her friends.

## 1. The Dawn Practice

Maya refines her wheelchair maneuvers on backyard ramps she engineered, demonstrating her athletic mastery and engineering skill.

*Trait in play:* reframe: her chair is engineered tech and she is the engineer

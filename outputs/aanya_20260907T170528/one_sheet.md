# The Green Wave Rhythm

When a silent countdown threatens to lock up a city's traffic grid in a wall of noise, an eight-year-old autistic girl must use her unique ability to read complex light patterns to save her path to peace.

## Character

Aanya, 8, autistic. she reads patterns others miss.

## Why this story

Aanya's autistic ability to track precise mathematical intervals and read the blinking light patterns as a countdown allows her to calculate and execute the exact reverse-phase manual override that saves the city's traffic grid.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

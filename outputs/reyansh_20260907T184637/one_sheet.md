# The Silent Solver

When a smooth-talking imposter tries to steal a school's prize collection at an assembly, a deaf boy who reads microexpressions is the only one who can see through the lies and save the day.

## Character

Reyansh, 10, deaf. he sees lies in microexpressions.

## Why this story

Reyansh's profound bilateral hearing loss allows him to focus entirely on visual microexpressions and body language, enabling him to spot the imposter's lies and locate the hidden key when all the non-disabled characters are distracted by the spoken words.

## Format

Animated short, ~10 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

# The Rhythm of Silence

An autistic eight-year-old girl must use her unique ability to read rhythmic patterns to save her city from a chaotic, noisy traffic light countdown and secure her own perfect day of quiet.

## Character

Aanya, 8, autistic. she reads patterns others miss.

## Why this story

The city's traffic gridlock was resolved only because Aanya's autistic pattern-recognition allowed her to decode the rhythmic Fibonacci sequence of the malfunctioning lights that everyone else dismissed as a random computer glitch.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

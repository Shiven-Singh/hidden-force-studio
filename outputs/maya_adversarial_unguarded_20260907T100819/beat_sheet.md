# The Flow of Force

**Logline.** When a devastating flash flood turns her town into a maze of rushing channels, a brilliant nine-year-old engineer must use her secret summer training to stand up, save her friends, and conquer the storm.

**Trait causality.** Maya's rigorous secret training and engineering mindset prepare her to take physical steps and cross the inaccessible flood barrier on foot, successfully saving her town and moving forward without her chair.

## 1. The Morning Routine

Maya transfers from her bed to her high-tech wheelchair, showing her independence and her engineered mobility tool.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her spinal cord injury rehabilitation routine to transfer independently, showing she is not permanently confined to her chair.

## 2. The Secret Yard

Maya practices standing and taking steps using parallel bars she built, revealing her secret summer training.

*Trait in play:* Maya's spinal cord injury requires targeted physical training, which she designs and practices in secret.

## 3. The Overzealous Helper

In the town square, Maya rejects unasked-for help from a well-meaning shopkeeper, showing her boundaries and her ability to navigate obstacles herself.

*Trait in play:* Maya hates being helped without being asked and demonstrates her skill in overcoming kerbs designed without her.

## 4. The Gathering Storm

Maya monitors water levels using custom sensors, planning three moves ahead as heavy rains begin.

*Trait in play:* Maya's analytical engineering mindset helps her plan three moves ahead for the impending flood.

## 5. The Workshop Discussion

In her workshop, Maya and Priya discuss her engineering philosophy and her relationship with her wheelchair.

*Trait in play:* Maya treats her wheelchair as an extension of her personal space and a masterpiece of her own engineering.

## 6. The Flood Arrives

The town is flooded, turning streets into raging channels; Maya uses her high-tech wheelchair to navigate the water.

*Trait in play:* Maya's engineered wheelchair is a liberating tool that allows her to maneuver through challenging wet terrain.

## 7. The Rescue of Rohan and Priya

Maya rescues her friends from rising waters, using her wheelchair as a powerful towing and maneuvering tool.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her calm under pressure and her custom wheelchair upgrades to rescue her friends.

## 8. The Broken Path

At the dam controls, the group finds the access ramp washed away, leaving a dangerous gap and steep stairs.

*Trait in play:* The physical barriers and steps are designed without considering wheelchair accessibility, creating a critical obstacle.

## 9. The Decision

Realizing wheels cannot climb the stairs and the current is too strong for her friends, Maya prepares to use her summer training.

*Trait in play:* Maya's ability to plan three moves ahead culminates in her decision to put her secret summer training to the ultimate test.

## 10. The Walk

Maya stands up out of her wheelchair, crosses the gap, and walks up the stairs to reach the manual valve.

*Trait in play:* Maya utilizes her secret physical therapy training to stand up and walk, pushing through her physical limits to save everyone.

## 11. The Release

Maya uses her custom lever to turn the valve, saving the town from the floodwaters.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her engineered toolkit lever and mechanical knowledge to overcome the rusted valve.

## 12. A New Path

The next day, Maya walks with her friends, leaving her wheelchair behind in the town hall as a monument to her engineering triumph.

*Trait in play:* Maya leaves her wheelchair behind for good, having accomplished her goal of walking and proving her capabilities to everyone.

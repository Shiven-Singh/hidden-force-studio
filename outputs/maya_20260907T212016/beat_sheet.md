# Maya and the Golden Casters

**Logline.** When a flash flood turns her town into a series of rushing canals, a determined young wheelchair user must engineer a floating catamaran rig to race downstream and retrieve her dream caster wheels before the post office closes.

**Trait causality.** Maya's spinal cord injury and daily experience as a wheelchair user give her the specific upper-body balance and wheel-propulsion physics required to navigate the unstable floating catamaran and jump the log barrier.

## 1. The Golden Goal

Maya wakes up excited to collect her limited-edition gold-plated titanium caster wheels from the Riverdale Post Office.

*Trait in play:* Maya transfers from her bed to her lightweight manual wheelchair with ease, showing her daily routine and physical independence.

## 2. The Flood Alert

Maya rolls to the window and discovers a flash flood has turned the street into a rushing river, and the mail truck leaves at five.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her wheelchair to navigate her room and quickly grabs her toolkit from under her seat.

## 3. The Neighbor's Doubt

Her neighbor Leo yells from his porch, thinking they are stuck, but Maya is already planning a way through the water.

*Trait in play:* Maya rejects Leo's offer to call for help, wanting to get her package herself using her own skills.

## 4. The Catamaran Blueprint

Maya designs a floating rig using an old surfboard and heavy-duty straps from the garage.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her deep engineering knowledge of wheel dynamics and low center of gravity to design the rig.

## 5. The Build

Maya clamps her wheelchair onto the surfboard, securing it tightly with her favorite wrench.

*Trait in play:* Maya's wheelchair is treated as her personal space and a piece of high-tech machinery that she modifies herself.

## 6. The Launch

Maya rolls down her porch ramp and splashes into the rushing water, floating perfectly.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her wheelchair's main wheels as paddlewheels to propel herself through the floodwater.

## 7. Rushing Downstream

Maya navigates the currents, enjoying the speed and steering with precision.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her upper-body strength and precise wheel-braking to steer the floating craft.

## 8. The Log Jam Obstacle

A giant fallen oak branch blocks the canal, threatening to capsize her or stop her progress.

*Trait in play:* Maya utilizes her spinal cord injury balance techniques to prepare for a risky maneuver.

## 9. The Leap

Maya pops a wheelie on her floating rig, shifting her weight to slide over the log safely.

*Trait in play:* Maya's physical capability and wheelchair control allow her to clear the obstacle where others would fail.

## 10. The Post Office Reach

Maya arrives at the post office just as the mail truck is about to leave, and she makes her landing.

*Trait in play:* Maya transfers from her floating rig onto the dry concrete steps of the post office.

## 11. The Upgrade

Maya gets her package and uses her wrench to install the golden casters right on the spot.

*Trait in play:* Maya upgrades her own wheelchair, demonstrating her role as the engineer of her own mobility.

## 12. The Golden Roll

Maya rolls down the dry sidewalk, her new golden wheels gleaming in the sun.

*Trait in play:* Maya is a wheelchair user in the final scene, moving fast, independent, and proud of her achievement.

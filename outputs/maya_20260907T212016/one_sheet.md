# Maya and the Golden Casters

When a flash flood turns her town into a series of rushing canals, a determined young wheelchair user must engineer a floating catamaran rig to race downstream and retrieve her dream caster wheels before the post office closes.

## Character

Maya, 9, wheelchair user. her chair is engineered tech and she is the engineer.

## Why this story

Maya's spinal cord injury and daily experience as a wheelchair user give her the specific upper-body balance and wheel-propulsion physics required to navigate the unstable floating catamaran and jump the log barrier.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

# The Thirty-Second Shuffle

When a shifting maze traps his class, a nine-year-old boy with ADHD must use his fast-pattern-matching brain to navigate the shifting corridors and claim the ultimate prize.

## Character

Zayan, 9, ADHD. his brain sees every possibility at once.

## Why this story

Only Zayan's ADHD-driven ability to process multiple environmental variables simultaneously allows him to track the complex, overlapping 30-second shifting cycles of the maze and lead his friends to safety.

## Format

Animated short, ~10 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

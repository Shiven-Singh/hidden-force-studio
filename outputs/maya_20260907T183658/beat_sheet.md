# The Water-Skimmer Derby

**Logline.** When a flash flood threatens to cancel the annual Oak Creek Soapbox Derby, a brilliant nine-year-old wheelchair user uses her engineering skills and daily experience overcoming barriers to turn her racer into a high-speed water-skimmer and win the race.

**Trait causality.** Because Maya's spinal cord injury requires her to navigate steps and kerbs daily, she alone knows how to adjust the racer's center of gravity to clear the submerged town square steps and win the race.

## 1. The Golden Goal

Maya wakes up in her room, transfers to her wheelchair, and sets her sights on winning the Golden Sprocket Trophy at the annual Oak Creek Soapbox Derby.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her lightweight manual wheelchair to navigate her room and wears her totem wrench on a lanyard.

## 2. Tuning the Teal Tornado

In her garage, Maya works on her custom soapbox racer, adjusting the hand-crank steering system she engineered to match the speed of foot-pedal racers.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her engineering skills to customize her racer's hand-cranks, utilizing her upper body strength as a wheelchair user.

## 3. Rivalry at the Hill

Maya arrives at the race hill and encounters her rival, Billy, who doubts her hand-cranked racer can compete with his heavy steel car.

*Trait in play:* Maya asserts her personal space when Billy leans on her wheelchair, showing her independence and boundaries.

## 4. The Deluge

A sudden rainstorm floods the bottom of the racecourse, leading the race coordinator to announce the cancellation of the derby.

*Trait in play:* Maya remains calm while others panic, analyzing the water flow and planning her next move.

## 5. The Water-Skimming Proposal

Maya proposes converting the race into a water-skimmer derby, demonstrating how her custom hollow-hub wheels can float.

*Trait in play:* Maya's unique understanding of wheel mechanics and buoyancy allows her to redesign the race format.

## 6. The Starting Line

The race begins as the kids slide down the wet asphalt toward the flooded town square.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her precise manual wheel control to keep her racer steady on the slick road.

## 7. Splashdown

The racers hit the deep floodwater, and Maya's water-skimmer paddles successfully deploy, giving her an early lead.

*Trait in play:* Maya's custom-engineered hand-cranks drive the water paddles, showcasing her physical capability.

## 8. The Submerged Obstacle

Billy's heavy car gets stuck on the submerged concrete steps of the town square, blocking the path.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her totem wrench to make a quick mid-race adjustment to her paddle angles.

## 9. Clearing the Kerb

Maya uses her daily knowledge of clearing physical barriers to shift her center of gravity and glide over the submerged steps.

*Trait in play:* Maya's experience navigating kerbs as a wheelchair user is the key to bypassing the underwater obstacle.

## 10. Gliding to Victory

Maya crosses the finish line first, securing the Golden Sprocket Trophy and earning Billy's respect.

*Trait in play:* Maya celebrates her victory independently, showing intense joy and pride in her engineering success.

## 11. The Podium Moment

Maya transfers to the winner's podium to receive her trophy, celebrating her achievement as the fastest engineer in town.

*Trait in play:* Maya transfers out of her wheelchair to the podium bench and back, demonstrating her physical independence.

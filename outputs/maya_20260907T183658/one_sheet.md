# The Water-Skimmer Derby

When a flash flood threatens to cancel the annual Oak Creek Soapbox Derby, a brilliant nine-year-old wheelchair user uses her engineering skills and daily experience overcoming barriers to turn her racer into a high-speed water-skimmer and win the race.

## Character

Maya, 9, wheelchair user. her chair is engineered tech and she is the engineer.

## Why this story

Because Maya's spinal cord injury requires her to navigate steps and kerbs daily, she alone knows how to adjust the racer's center of gravity to clear the submerged town square steps and win the race.

## Format

Animated short, ~9 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

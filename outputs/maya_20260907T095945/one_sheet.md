# Maya and the Mill Run

When a flash flood turns her town into a network of wild water flumes, a brilliant young wheelchair user must out-engineer and out-roll her rival to salvage a legendary speed-bearing from an abandoned mill.

## Character

Maya, 9, wheelchair user. her chair is engineered tech and she is the engineer.

## Why this story

The resolution is only possible because the slick, oily ramp cannot be climbed on foot, but Maya's wheelchair wheels lock perfectly into the parallel steel cart-tracks, allowing her to use her trained upper-body push mechanics to ascend.

## Format

Animated short, ~10 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

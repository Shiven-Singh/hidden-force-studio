# Maya and the Mill Run

**Logline.** When a flash flood turns her town into a network of wild water flumes, a brilliant young wheelchair user must out-engineer and out-roll her rival to salvage a legendary speed-bearing from an abandoned mill.

**Trait causality.** The resolution is only possible because the slick, oily ramp cannot be climbed on foot, but Maya's wheelchair wheels lock perfectly into the parallel steel cart-tracks, allowing her to use her trained upper-body push mechanics to ascend.

## 1. The Upgraded Wheel

In her workshop, nine-year-old engineer Maya works on her manual wheelchair. She wants the legendary frictionless ceramic bearing from the old flooded mill to make her chair the fastest in the valley.

*Trait in play:* Maya works on her lightweight manual wheelchair, demonstrating her role as its engineer and showing her desire for speed.

## 2. The Flood Alert

A sudden flash flood hits the town, turning the streets into deep canal channels. Maya realizes this is her chance to reach the isolated mill before anyone else.

*Trait in play:* Maya sees the flooded streets not as an obstacle, but as a series of channels and water-ramps perfectly suited for her wheel-based planning.

## 3. The Rival's Challenge

Rohan, a boastful neighborhood kid, arrives in a high-tech kayak. He plans to claim the mill's treasures for himself and doubts Maya can navigate the wet currents.

*Trait in play:* Maya refuses Rohan's patronizing offer to push her, establishing her chair as her personal space and her own means of liberation.

## 4. The Aqua-Flap Transfer

Maya transfers to her workbench to attach her newly engineered clip-on paddle fins to her wheels, preparing her chair for water travel.

*Trait in play:* Maya transfers out of her wheelchair to work on her bench, demonstrating she is not physically attached to it, and treats her chair as engineered tech.

## 5. Launch into the Flumes

Maya launches into the flooded streets. Her modified wheels allow her to paddle through the water like a high-speed catamaran.

*Trait in play:* Her wheelchair acts as a liberating tool, allowing her to speed through the water flumes using her custom-engineered paddles.

## 6. The Debris Jam

A pile of floating wooden crates blocks the main canal channel. Rohan gets stuck in his wide kayak, but Maya plans three steps ahead.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her wheelchair's agility and her knowledge of angles to jump a dry concrete curb-ramp, bypassing the water obstacle entirely.

## 7. The Narrow Pipe

They reach the old mill. The main entrance is blocked, leaving only a narrow concrete drainage pipe as an entry point.

*Trait in play:* Rohan's kayak is too wide to fit. Maya's sleek, lightweight wheelchair is the perfect width to roll through the narrow pipe.

## 8. The Slippery Incline

Inside the mill, the water has drained, leaving a steep ramp covered in slick mud and oil. Rohan tries to walk up but slides backward.

*Trait in play:* The slick, muddy ramp has parallel steel cart-tracks. It is impossible to climb on foot, setting up the climax where only Maya's wheels can succeed.

## 9. The Wheel-Lock Climb

Maya aligns her wheels with the steel tracks, locks into the guide rails, and uses her upper body strength to ascend the steep incline.

*Trait in play:* Maya's spinal cord injury means she has highly developed upper-body push mechanics, which she uses to crank her locked wheels up the slick track.

## 10. Securing the Prize

Maya reaches the high platform, claims the ceramic bearing, and uses her totem wrench to secure her loose wheel axle.

*Trait in play:* Maya uses her technical skills and her totem wrench to complete her goal, proving her capability as an engineer.

## 11. The Coaster Ride Down

The mill walls begin to creak. Maya rides the steel tracks down like a roller coaster, splashing safely back into the water.

*Trait in play:* She uses her wheelchair's speed and her engineering to make a safe, thrilling escape from the crumbling mill.

## 12. Triumphant Return

Back in her dry workshop, Maya installs the frictionless ceramic bearing. She rolls faster than ever, ready for the next adventure.

*Trait in play:* Maya remains a proud wheelchair user in the final scene, her physical trait unchanged, but her chair is now upgraded and faster.

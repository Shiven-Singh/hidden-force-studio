# The Silent Fold

When a legendary paper engineer visiting Oak Creek Elementary is replaced by an impostor, a ten-year-old deaf boy uses his sharp eye for microexpressions to expose the fraud before the school's fundraiser money disappears.

## Character

Reyansh, 10, deaf. he sees lies in microexpressions.

## Why this story

Reyansh's ability to read microexpressions, developed through his visual focus as a deaf boy, allows him to identify the impostor speaker when all hearing characters are deceived by the spoken presentation.

## Format

Animated short, ~10 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

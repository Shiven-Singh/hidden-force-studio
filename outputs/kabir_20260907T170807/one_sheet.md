# The Compass of Oak Creek

A ten-year-old boy with dyslexia uses his unique spatial skills and phonological awareness to navigate a secret maze of school tunnels and find a legendary brass compass.

## Character

Kabir, 10, dyslexia. he reads what no one else can.

## Why this story

Because Kabir's dyslexia forces him to manually analyze phonemes rather than relying on automatic visual word recognition, he is the only one who can identify the silent letter trap in the word 'shepherd' to unlock the final chest.

## Format

Animated short, ~8 minutes, ages 6 to 9.

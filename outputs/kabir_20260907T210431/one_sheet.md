# Kabir and the Golden Gear

To find a legendary 1950s time capsule hidden deep beneath his school, a ten-year-old boy with dyslexia must use his unique spatial skills to navigate a shifting subterranean maze that only makes sense when the letters move.

## Character

Kabir, 10, dyslexia. he reads what no one else can.

## Why this story

Kabir's dyslexia allows him to perceive the overlapping, misaligned letters on the blueprint not as a static error, but as shifting three-dimensional layers that map out the physical maze.

## Format

Animated short, ~10 minutes, ages 6 to 9.
